# Problem 3: Count Even and Odd Numbers

## Problem
Given `n` integers, count how many are even and how many are odd.

## Concept
A number is even when its remainder after division by 2 is zero. Otherwise, it is odd. Scan each element once and update one of two counters.

## Example
```text
Input:
5
2 7 4 9 10
Output:
Even: 3
Odd: 2
```

## Complexity
Time O(n); extra space O(1), excluding the input array.
